Previe Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Previe is a grid planner for iPhone made by Gannon Freeman. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Previe does not collect any of your data. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What Previe stores, and where

Previe keeps the photos you add, your previews, crops, captions, plans and settings in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Previe has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

Photos, camera and microphone

Previe asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Photos: to choose photos to preview, and (with full access) to let the next-post finder look at your recent photos on the device. Photos are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Add to Photos: to save a framed photo when you tap Ready to post.
  • Camera and microphone: to capture a photo or Reel for a preview.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Loading your profile

If you choose Load Instagram, Previe opens Instagram's own website inside the app. You sign in, if needed, directly with Instagram. Previe reads only the profile details and post thumbnails already shown on that page and saves copies on your iPhone. It never reads or stores your password or cookies and never posts anything. Your use of Instagram's website is covered by Instagram's own privacy policy.

You can also import your Instagram data download, or choose photos yourself. Those files are processed on your iPhone.

Notifications

If you plan a post, Previe can schedule a reminder notification. Reminders are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.

Sharing to Previe

The "Send to Previe" share option copies the photos you share into Previe's storage on your iPhone.

Backups

If you export a project backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Previe does not send it anywhere on its own.

Children

Previe does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
