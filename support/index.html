PrevieGrid planner for iPhone

Previe Support

Previe shows your next photo in your real profile grid before you post it. Everything stays on your iPhone, and nothing is ever published for you.

Getting started

  • Tap Load Instagram and sign in on Instagram's own page if it asks. Tap Load 10 Rows, then Import.
  • Tap + to add the photo you're thinking of posting. It appears at the top left, where your next post will go.
  • Tap and hold a preview to move it. Tap Compare to see your grid with and without previews.

Common questions

A post appears twice, or my grid doesn't line up with my profile.

Open Settings › Look and layout › Reset grid. It removes extra copies, restores newest-first order and clears custom spacing and crop. "Reset, then reload from Instagram" re-reads your rows from the top of your profile. Undo brings the previous grid back.

Does Previe post to my account?

No. Ready to post copies your caption and saves the framed photo; you post it yourself.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Use Settings › More tools › Export project backup to keep a copy.

Instagram shows only some of my posts.

Instagram shows more posts after you sign in on its page. Then tap Load 10 More Rows.

I hid something and can't find it.

Tap with three fingers to bring back the last thing you hid, or open Settings › Hide things.
